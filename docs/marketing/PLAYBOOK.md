# BinaApp Marketing Playbook

Everything here costs time, not money. At RM 5 a month a paid ad can never
pay for itself, so the plan is: show real restaurant owners their own
website before asking for anything, film it, and measure every link.

## 1. Tracked links — use these, never the bare address

Every link you share should say where it was shared. The founder dashboard
(`/founder` → **Marketing**) then shows visitors, signups and paying
customers per source.

| Where you post it | Link |
|---|---|
| TikTok bio | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio` |
| A specific TikTok video | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=NAMA-VIDEO` |
| Instagram bio | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` |
| Facebook group post | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=NAMA-GROUP` |
| WhatsApp outreach | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_campaign=outreach` |
| WhatsApp status | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_campaign=status` |
| Printed flyer / QR | `https://binaapp.my/?utm_source=flyer&utm_campaign=NAMA-KAWASAN` |

Rules: lowercase, no spaces (use `-`), one `utm_campaign` per video or
group so you can tell which one worked.

## 2. Outreach: "website kedai tuan dah siap"

The routine, 10 kedai a day, about an hour:

1. Pick a kedai makan that is active on Facebook/Instagram or Google Maps
   and has **no website** (check their bio and Maps listing).
2. Build their site in BinaApp from their public menu and photos. Do not
   publish it; open the preview and screen-record 15 seconds of scrolling.
3. Send message A with the recording. Never send a bare link first; a
   video of *their own* kedai gets opened, a link from a stranger does not.
4. Log it: kedai name, date, replied or not, signed up or not.
5. No reply after 3 days: send message B once. Then stop.

Only message business numbers the kedai advertises publicly, once plus one
follow-up, and stop immediately if they say no.

### Message A — first contact

> Assalamualaikum tuan/puan, saya Yassir dari BinaApp.
>
> Saya nampak [NAMA KEDAI] belum ada website, jadi saya cuba buatkan satu.
> Ini rakaman dia (15 saat). Menu dan gambar saya ambil dari page tuan.
>
> Pelanggan boleh tengok menu dan terus order ke WhatsApp tuan. Tiada
> komisen macam app delivery.
>
> Kalau tuan suka, RM 5 sebulan je untuk terbitkan. Kalau tak berminat pun
> tak apa, saya cuma nak tunjuk. 🙏

### Message B — follow-up after 3 days

> Salam tuan/puan, saya yang hantar video website [NAMA KEDAI] hari tu.
> Website tu masih saya simpan. Nak saya betulkan apa-apa dulu (harga,
> gambar, waktu buka) sebelum tuan tengok semula?

### Message C — when they ask "berapa?"

> RM 5 sebulan, tuan. Itu dah termasuk website, menu, butang order
> WhatsApp dan alamat sendiri: [namakedai].binaapp.my.
> Tiada kontrak, boleh berhenti bila-bila. Tiada komisen setiap order.
>
> Kalau tuan ada rider sendiri dan nak sistem delivery penuh dengan GPS,
> itu pelan Bisnes RM 49 sebulan.

### Message D — when they say "saya dah guna GrabFood / Foodpanda"

> Bagus tu tuan, teruskan. Website ni bukan ganti, dia untuk pelanggan
> tetap tuan. Order dari website masuk terus WhatsApp tuan, jadi untuk
> pelanggan yang dah kenal kedai, tuan tak perlu bayar komisen.
> Ramai letak QR website di meja dan dalam beg bungkus.

### Message E — after they sign up (ask for the referral)

> Terima kasih tuan! Website dah live: [LINK].
> Satu permintaan kecil: kalau ada kawan peniaga yang nak website macam
> ni, boleh kenalkan saya? Saya buatkan dulu, percuma tengok.

### Facebook group post (peniaga / F&B groups — read the group rules first)

> Saya tengah bina website percuma-tengok untuk 10 kedai makan minggu ni.
>
> Tuan/puan cuma komen nama kedai + link menu. Saya buatkan website
> lengkap dengan menu dan butang order WhatsApp, dan hantar rakaman.
> Suka, RM 5 sebulan untuk terbitkan. Tak suka, tak payah bayar apa-apa.
>
> Contoh yang dah siap: [LINK CONTOH]

## 3. TikTok / Reels — 30 days

One format carries most of it: **build a real kedai's website on camera**.
Keep every video 20–35 seconds, vertical, with on-screen text (most people
watch muted). End every video the same way:

> Text on screen: **"Nak website kedai anda? Link di bio."**

Shooting notes:

- First 2 seconds decide everything: show the finished website or the
  food, then the hook line as big text.
- Screen-record the phone, not a laptop. Owners use phones.
- Get the owner's permission before naming or showing a real kedai. If you
  don't have it, build for a made-up kedai and say so.
- Post between 12–1 pm or 8–10 pm. Reply to every comment the same day.
- Reuse each video on Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels unchanged.

### Week 1 — the 60-second build

| Day | Hook (first line on screen) | What you show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Saya bina website untuk warung ni dalam 60 saat" | Type the description in BM, timer on screen, reveal the site |
| 2 | "Kedai nasi lemak ni tak ada website. Sekarang ada." | Before: only a Facebook page. After: site with menu |
| 3 | "RM 5 sebulan. Ini apa yang anda dapat." | Scroll one finished site top to bottom, label each part |
| 4 | "Saya taip dalam Manglish je, tengok AI faham ke tak" | Type a messy casual brief, show the result |
| 5 | "Pelanggan order dari website, masuk terus WhatsApp" | Add to cart → WhatsApp message arrives on a second phone |
| 6 | "Gambar makanan pun AI yang buat" | Menu item with no photo → generated photo |
| 7 | "Minggu ni saya bina 7 website. Ini semua." | Fast montage of the week's sites |

### Week 2 — the commission problem

| Day | Hook | What you show |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | "Jual RM 1,000, platform ambil sampai RM 300" | Simple maths on screen, then "website sendiri: RM 5 sebulan" |
| 9 | "Pelanggan tetap anda patut order terus dengan anda" | Regular customer scans table QR, orders direct |
| 10 | "QR di meja, pelanggan scan, order sampai dapur" | Print the QR poster, stick it on a table, scan |
| 11 | "Ada rider sendiri? Anda tak perlukan app orang lain" | Rider GPS tracking screen (Bisnes plan) |
| 12 | "Website ke Instagram? Jawapan saya" | Talking head: IG is for finding you, website is for ordering |
| 13 | "3 benda pelanggan cari sebelum datang kedai anda" | Menu with prices, waktu buka, lokasi — show all three on a site |
| 14 | "Berapa kos website kalau upah orang?" | Compare a designer quote range with RM 5 a month |

### Week 3 — show the free extras

| Day | Hook | What you show |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | "Poster QR kedai anda, siap untuk print" | Generate the A4 QR poster |
| 16 | "Menu A4 terus dari website" | Print-ready menu from the site's own data |
| 17 | "Kad setia: beli 9 percuma 1" | Print the loyalty stamp cards, cut, stamp one |
| 18 | "Nak lebih review Google? Letak ni di kaunter" | The 5-star review QR poster |
| 19 | "Tukar warna website dalam 1 saat" | Design Studio: shuffle palettes and fonts |
| 20 | "Website gaya kartun pun boleh" | Doodle style site for a kids' bakery or cafe |
| 21 | "Kedai tutup cuti? Ada poster untuk tu" | Holiday closure poster with reopen date |

### Week 4 — proof and requests

| Day | Hook | What you show |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | "Komen nama kedai anda, saya bina website dia" | Call for requests; this feeds next week's videos |
| 23 | "Saya bina website untuk [kedai dari komen]" | Reply-to-comment video |
| 24 | "Tauke kedai ni tengok website dia buat kali pertama" | Owner's real reaction (with permission) |
| 25 | "Order pertama dari website" | A real first order notification (with permission) |
| 26 | "Soalan paling ramai tanya: susah ke nak guna?" | Edit a price on the phone in 10 seconds |
| 27 | "Bukan kedai makan je" | Salon, bengkel, homestay built with the same steps |
| 28 | "Saya bina website untuk [kedai dari komen] #2" | Reply-to-comment video |
| 29 | "Kenapa saya buat BinaApp" | 30-second founder story, face on camera |
| 30 | "30 hari, 30 video. Ini yang jadi." | Honest results: views, signups, what worked |

After day 30, open `/founder` → Marketing, sort by signups, and make more
of the three videos that brought the most signups. Drop the rest.

## 4. Weekly check — 10 minutes every Monday

1. `/founder` → Marketing → 7 days. Write down visitors, signups, paying.
2. Which source brought the most **signups** (not visitors)?
3. Outreach log: how many sent, replied, signed up?
4. Do more of the best source next week. Stop the worst one.

Targets for the first month: 300 kedai contacted, 30 videos posted,
30 new signups, 10 paying.
